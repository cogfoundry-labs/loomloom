The prompt

The exact brief this run used. Copy it, or adapt it.

Goal: Create an information-dense anime storyboard / image-generation sheet for a 30-second One Piece-style scene titled ワンピース　30秒用 絵コンテ「ゾロとロビンの静かな会話」, showing a quiet emotional conversation between Zoro and Robin. Canvas: Landscape 16:9 white storyboard page with thin black borders, clean grid lines, and Japanese typography. Add a small top-right note reading "尺：30秒　解像度：16:9". Use the look of a production storyboard sheet: crisp black frame boxes, white caption bands under each frame, and anime screenshots inside the boxes. Layout: exactly 10 numbered storyboard panels plus 2 text note boxes. Panels 1-4 across the top row, panels 5-8 across the middle row, panel 9 at bottom left, panel 10 wide at bottom center, and two note boxes on the bottom right. Each panel: a circled number, a time range, a cinematic anime still, and a Japanese caption below it. Panels: 1. "① 0:00-0:03" Wide night shot, blue tones: Zoro and Robin sit apart among ancient stone ruins and broken columns. Caption: ロビン「…また迷ってるの？」 2. "② 0:03-0:06" Close-up of Zoro facing forward, green hair, stern expression, one eye closed, earrings, dark green robe. Caption: ゾロ「…別に」 3. "③ 0:06-0:10" Close-up of Robin resting her cheek on her hand, long black hair, gentle knowing smile, purple outfit, stone wall. Caption: ロビン「強いからって、全部ひとりで背負わなくていいのよ」 4. "④ 0:10-0:13" Side profile of Zoro in warm low light against stone ruins, serious. Caption: ゾロ「背負うつもりはねぇ」 5. "⑤ 0:13-0:16" Close-up of Robin smiling softly, wind in her hair, cloudy sky. Caption: ロビン「じゃあ…信じてるのね、仲間を」 6. "⑥ 0:16-0:19" Side close-up of Zoro looking down with a small smile, leaves blowing, daylight. Caption: ゾロ「ああ。てめェがいるからな」 7. "⑦ 0:19-0:23" Medium two-shot, bright daylight: Robin and Zoro by a stone balcony railing, blue sky, drifting leaves. Caption: ロビン「フフ…心強いわ」 8. "⑧ 0:23-0:26" Close-up of Zoro grinning confidently, one eye narrowed, gold earrings, green hair, wind-blown leaves. Caption: ゾロ「当たり前だ」 9. "⑨ 0:26-0:28" Close-up of Robin, eyes closed, serene smile, pink flower petals drifting. Caption: ロビン「ありがとう、ゾロ」 10. "⑩ 0:28-0:30" Wide sunset shot from behind: Robin and Zoro at a balcony overlooking the ocean, purple-orange sky. Caption: （無言で、同じ未来を見つめる二人） Note boxes (bottom right, exactly 2): Box 1 title 演出メモ, bullets: 全体のトーン：静かで信頼感のある会話劇 / BGM：しっとりとしたピアノメインの感動系 / SE：風の音、波の音を控えめに / セリフは間を大切に、表情の「間」で魅せる, final line: ラストカットは余韻を大切に. Box 2 title キャラクター設定: ゾロ：クールだが仲間想い、言葉は少ないが真っ直ぐ / ロビン：落ち着いていて優しい、仲間を信じて支える強さ. Visual style: high-quality hand-drawn Japanese TV anime aesthetic, clean cel shading, expressive faces, cinematic lighting, blue night ruins to bright sky to warm sunset. Zoro: short green hair, three gold earrings, a scarred closed eye, muscular build, dark green clothing. Robin: long black hair, elegant features, calm eyes, purple patterned top. Constraints: all visible text in Japanese as specified, precise readable grid, exactly 10 panels, exactly 2 note boxes, no watermarks, no logos, no photorealism.
