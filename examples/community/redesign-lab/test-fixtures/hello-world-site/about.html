About Us

About us

Hello World Widgets started on a workbench, fixing the same small part over and over until we decided to just make a better one. We are a small team that still tests every batch by hand before it ships.

We keep our catalog short on purpose: fewer parts, each one built to last, rather than a wall of options nobody actually needs.

How we work

Every design starts from a real repair somebody on the team ran into. We prototype it, use it ourselves for a season, then open it up to customers once we would trust it on our own projects.

Feedback goes straight back to the bench. If a part fails in the field, the next batch is already different.
