The initial color system employed a balanced range of light and dark greys, alongside primary blue and secondary green tones. This was subsequently redefined to a high-contrast scheme featuring a pure black background and a bright foreground. The updated palette emphasizes a vibrant phosphor green and distinct reds for alerts and rules.
