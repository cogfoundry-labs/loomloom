BRAND NAME = adidas
PRODUCT = adidas Adizero running shoe
Act as a Social Media Art Director and Digital Collage Artist specializing in bold, youth-oriented sports brand content for Instagram and digital campaigns.
PHASE 1: CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK
Create a dynamic digital collage that merges high-performance running photography with contemporary urban graphic design.
This is technical speed breaking into street energy — a composition that feels fast, precise, confident and expressive while maintaining strong adidas brand coherence.
Aesthetic direction:

* Technical but human
* Raw urban textures
* Layered imperfections
* Youth-driven visual energy
* High confidence, high movement
* Performance precision mixed with street culture

Visual language:
Geometric fragments, technical grids, torn photographic edges, directional arrows, hand-drawn running marks, speed trails, track-inspired lines, cropped numbers and bold adidas-inspired graphic blocking.
The composition should feel deliberately art-directed rather than perfectly polished.
It should communicate speed, progress, individuality and forward movement, without becoming a generic sports advertisement.
PHASE 2: MODEL & PHOTOGRAPHY

* Subject: One elite adult runner, age 20–30
* Pose Energy: 80% attitude, 20% natural — explosive sprint, powerful stride, airborne transition or strong forward lean — NEVER static
* Outfit: Contemporary adidas performance running apparel
* Hero Product: One adidas Adizero running shoe, clearly visible and visually important
* Photography Style: Premium editorial sports photography cutout
* Background Removal: Clean, sharp extraction
* Camera Angle: Slight low angle, hero perspective
* Crop: Full body or 3/4 body, with the Adizero shoe clearly visible
* Body position: Strong diagonal movement through the frame
* Expression: Focused, confident, determined — never exaggerated or theatrical

The runner should look like a serious contemporary athlete rather than a fitness model.
The shoe must remain physically realistic, anatomically correctly positioned and commercially desirable.
PHASE 3: COLOR BLOCKING FOUNDATION

* Primary Foundation: Black, white and warm off-white / light grey
* Primary Color Blob: One large irregular graphic shape occupying approximately 40–60% of the frame
* Accent Color: One high-energy adidas performance accent such as electric blue, cobalt, solar yellow or vivid red
* Shape Style: Irregular, graphic, slightly imperfect — NO perfect geometry
* Texture: Subtle brush grain / print noise / paper texture
* Placement: Behind or partially wrapping around the runner, framing the athlete without hiding the runner or Adizero shoe

The color system should feel distinctly adidas rather than Nike.
Use restraint: black and white should establish the visual foundation, while the single accent color creates energy and directs the eye toward the athlete and shoe.
PHASE 4: GRAPHIC ELEMENTS LAYER
Add 3–5 carefully art-directed abstract graphic elements.
Element types:

* Hand-drawn speed marks
* Directional arrows
* Running trajectory lines
* Technical measurement symbols
* Cropped athletic numbers
* Track-lane or route-inspired lines
* Subtle technical grid fragments
* Small geometric impact marks
* Controlled photographic duplication or offset fragments
* Texture overlays such as dust, grain, paper and fabric noise

Rules:

* Colors: black + off-white + one energetic accent color
* Placement: Asymmetric, with strong top-left / bottom-right or bottom-left / top-right visual tension
* Scale: Mix small (5%) and medium (10–20%) elements with one larger graphic intervention
* Avoid center clutter
* Graphics must reinforce the runner's direction of movement
* Keep the visual language imperfect and contemporary
* Do not allow graphics to obscure the face, body or Adizero shoe

The graphics should feel like part of the campaign's visual identity, not decorative stickers added afterward.
PHASE 5: TYPOGRAPHY INTEGRATION

* adidas branding: Upper-left or upper-right, approximately 10–15% of frame width
* Primary Headline: RUN YOUR FAST.
* Supporting Copy: ADIZERO
* Optional small supporting descriptor: ENGINEERED FOR SPEED.
* Type Style: Bold condensed sans-serif / contemporary grotesk / athletic editorial typography
* Treatment: Mix large straight typography with one slightly rotated or cropped element
* Hierarchy: adidas branding → headline → product / supporting copy
* Typography should integrate with the graphic composition rather than simply sit on top of the image

The headline should feel confident and contemporary, not like a generic motivational slogan.
Use typography as a directional element that helps reinforce the diagonal movement of the runner.
PHASE 6: TEXTURE & BACKGROUND

* Base Layer: Warm off-white / light grey neutral
* Color Range: Approximately RGB 245–250 for the lightest areas
* Texture Options: Fine paper grain, fabric texture, subtle print noise, lightly distressed photographic texture
* Rule: Texture should be FELT, not dominant

Introduce enough physical imperfection to make the digital artwork feel tactile and human.
Avoid excessive grunge.
The final image should feel like a contemporary physical poster photographed or scanned into a digital campaign rather than a generic AI-generated graphic.
PHASE 7: COMPOSITION RULES

* Layout: Asymmetric balance
* Runner: Off-center
* Movement: Strong diagonal flow through the entire composition
* Breathing Room: Approximately 15–20% negative space
* Focal Point: Runner + Adizero shoe = approximately 60% of visual attention
* Graphics = approximately 40% supporting visual energy
* The runner must visually travel through the composition rather than simply stand inside it

Layering Order:
Background
→ Urban / technical texture
→ Primary color field
→ Graphic elements
→ Runner
→ Adizero product emphasis
→ Typography
→ Fine texture / print details
The composition should have a clear visual hierarchy.
The viewer should naturally notice:
runner → Adizero shoe → headline → adidas branding → supporting graphic details.
Do not make every part of the image equally loud.
PHASE 8: BRAND INTELLIGENCE — AUTO-ADAPT
Adapt the visual energy based on adidas's sports and cultural identity:
→ Performance: lightweight speed, technical precision, aerodynamic movement, product credibility
→ Running: personal progress, endurance, pace, forward motion and the pursuit of a faster self
→ Street Culture: contemporary urban energy, individuality, expressive graphics and fashion-aware styling
→ adidas Heritage: recognizable 3-Stripes language, strong graphic simplicity, confident black-and-white foundations and modern sport design
→ Youth Culture: raw textures, asymmetric layouts, expressive typography and controlled visual experimentation
The final image should feel unmistakably adidas without becoming a literal collection of logos and brand symbols.
Do NOT imitate Nike's visual identity.
Do NOT use Nike-specific slogans, visual motifs or branding.
The creative language should communicate adidas through its own combination of performance, sport, street culture and design.
PHASE 9: PRODUCT & BRAND INTEGRITY
The adidas Adizero shoe is a hero product, not merely an accessory.
Ensure:

* The shoe is clearly visible
* The shoe has realistic proportions
* The sole and upper structure are physically plausible
* The 3-Stripes are clean and recognizable
* The shoe receives sufficient lighting and contrast
* Graphic elements never accidentally deform or cover the product
* The shoe looks premium and commercially desirable

Avoid surreal product distortion.
Avoid extra shoes.
Avoid duplicate runners.
Avoid unnecessary objects that compete with the hero product.

The final visual should be a premium adidas Adizero running campaign poster combining an explosive editorial runner photograph, a clearly visible hero performance shoe, contemporary urban graphic design, technical running marks, asymmetric color blocking, expressive typography, raw tactile textures and strong directional movement.
The finished artwork should feel like a contemporary global adidas campaign discovered on a leading design platform — highly art-directed, energetic, editorial, technically credible, imperfect and youth-driven.

SPEED. PROGRESS. PERFORMANCE.
