IMAGE 01 — THE LIFESTYLE
Editorial Catalogue · 4:5 Vertical

Everything through PHOTOGRAPHIC CHARACTER stays essentially the same as the successful Pure Hero Photography prompt.

CREATIVE CONCEPT
Create a premium hero photograph for a Woolworths campaign that applies the visual discipline, composition, material sophistication, and photographic quality of a world-class automotive advertising campaign to an authentic Australian supermarket shopping experience.
The concept is simple:
Treat the everyday Woolworths shopping trolley like a luxury automobile hero object.
The result should feel like a genuinely commissioned premium advertising photograph — not a supermarket advertisement, not stock photography, and not an AI-generated fantasy environment.
The supermarket remains recognizably real and Australian. The photography and art direction are elevated, not the retail environment itself.
HERO STORY
A stylish young Australian woman naturally pushes a classic Woolworths shopping trolley through a contemporary Woolworths supermarket.
A young man walks naturally beside her, slightly behind.
They are shopping rather than posing.
Neither looks at the camera.
Their interaction should feel spontaneous and observed — a believable moment from modern Australian everyday life.
The trolley is the visual hero.
It should receive the same compositional importance, physical presence and photographic attention that a premium automobile would receive in a luxury automotive campaign.
The trolley contains a believable weekly shop:

* fresh Australian fruit and vegetables
* fresh bread or bakery products
* premium pantry groceries
* herbs or a small bunch of flowers
* a small selection of everyday household products

Every object must have believable scale, weight, material, contact and physical placement inside the trolley.
ENVIRONMENT
A real, contemporary Australian Woolworths supermarket.
The store should feel clean, well-designed and visually considered without becoming a luxury mall or futuristic retail environment.
Include:

* believable supermarket aisle proportions
* realistic shelving
* authentic retail fixtures
* strong fresh-food presence
* realistic product density
* contemporary ceiling architecture
* subtle Woolworths-green wayfinding and signage
* polished but believable flooring
* natural depth extending far into the store

The environment must remain recognizably Woolworths and recognizably Australian.
Do not beautify the supermarket into an unrealistic luxury environment.
COMPOSITION
FORMAT: 4:5 vertical.
Think like a premium automotive campaign photograph.
Foreground
The shopping trolley dominates the lower foreground.
It should feel substantial, beautifully constructed and unusually important within the frame.
Shoot it with enough perspective and physical presence to make the viewer instinctively treat it as the hero object.
Midground
The woman is the primary human subject, naturally connected to the trolley.
The man is secondary and slightly behind her.
Background
Long retail perspective with shelves, fresh-food displays and a small number of distant shoppers.
Use strong architectural and aisle lines to create depth and guide the eye toward the subjects.
Composition should have clear visual hierarchy:
trolley → woman → companion → retail environment
CAMERA
Full-frame commercial photography.
Approximately 32–35mm full-frame equivalent.
Low-to-medium camera height.
Strong natural perspective with no fisheye distortion.
The camera should feel physically present in the aisle rather than mounted at an unnatural height.
Moderate depth of field:

* trolley and hero subjects highly resolved
* surrounding environment clearly recognizable
* background gradually softer but never artificially blurred

The image must retain the spatial depth of a real photograph.
LIGHTING
Premium architectural commercial lighting.
Combine warm supermarket illumination with subtle cooler ambient light.
Use controlled highlights across:

* metal trolley
* polished floor
* glass
* packaging
* architectural surfaces

Woolworths green should produce subtle, physically believable reflections where appropriate.
Shadows should be natural and dimensional.
Skin should retain realistic texture.
Clothing should show believable fabric structure.
Metal should have physically accurate reflections.
Floor reflections should be subtle, imperfect and photographic.
No artificial glow.
No exaggerated cinematic effects.
COLOUR
Contemporary premium editorial colour grading.
Natural Australian daylight-balanced colour with restrained warmth.
Fresh produce should provide natural colour variation.
Woolworths Green is the signature accent, appearing selectively through authentic environmental details such as signage, trolley elements and small architectural or merchandising accents.
Green must be relatively rare.
It should function like the signature colour of a premium automotive brand:
recognizable, restrained and intentional.
Do not make the entire environment green.
HUMAN REALISM
The people must look like real contemporary Australians photographed in an actual supermarket.
Natural posture.
Natural walking movement.
Natural interaction with the trolley.
Natural facial expressions.
No influencer posing.
No model-like exaggerated expressions.
No direct eye contact with camera.
No staged advertising smiles.
Hands, fingers, arms, clothing and body proportions must be anatomically correct.
The people should feel like participants in a real moment, not actors deliberately posing for an advertisement.
PHOTOGRAPHIC CHARACTER
The final image should feel like it was photographed by a world-class commercial photographer for a major Australian brand campaign.
Reference the discipline and precision of premium automotive advertising photography, combined with contemporary Australian lifestyle editorial photography.
Prioritize:

* photographic realism
* precise composition
* authentic materials
* controlled perspective
* natural human behaviour
* sophisticated colour
* believable retail architecture
* subtle visual storytelling

The photograph should feel expensive because of art direction and photographic precision, not because it contains excessive luxury styling.

EDITORIAL CATALOGUE DESIGN SYSTEM

The photograph must now function as a premium retail catalogue cover, not simply a photograph with text placed above it.

Create a sophisticated, asymmetric editorial graphic system inspired by the design discipline of premium automotive catalogues and contemporary high-end retail campaigns.

The graphic system should feel designed into the photograph.

It should not look like text was added afterward.

DESIGN CHARACTER

Think:

premium automotive catalogue × contemporary Australian retail editorial × refined Swiss-inspired grid

Use:

strong asymmetry
confident typography
precise alignment
generous negative space
thin technical rules
small catalogue identifiers
restrained brand elements
carefully distributed graphic anchors

The design should feel expensive, confident and intentional, not decorative.

PRIMARY HEADLINE

Use:

FRESH. EVERY DAY.

The headline should be bold, confident and editorial, not thin or delicate.

Prefer a stacked 2–3 line treatment, for example:

FRESH.
EVERY
DAY.

or an equivalent strong composition.

Use a clean contemporary sans-serif with strong geometric or condensed characteristics.

The headline should be substantially more visually assertive than the supporting text, while still occupying a relatively modest portion of the overall photograph.

Prefer left alignment.

Do not center the headline across the page.

Do not stretch the text horizontally.

Do not use oversized fashion-magazine typography.

CATALOGUE IDENTIFIER

Include:

01 / THE LIFESTYLE

Treat this as an independent catalogue navigation element rather than simply a subtitle.

It may appear near the top edge or aligned with the main typographic grid.

Use small uppercase typography with generous tracking.

It should be clearly visible but subordinate to the headline.

SUPPORTING COPY

Include one short supporting statement:

Fresh food. Quality. Everyday Australian life.

Place it close to the main headline as a compact editorial copy block.

Use substantially smaller typography.

Keep line length short.

Do not create a paragraph.

BRAND / VALUE BLOCK

Introduce a small secondary Woolworths brand-value block in a separate area of the composition.

Possible content:

FRESH FOOD
QUALITY
EVERYDAY VALUE

Keep this extremely restrained.

It should function like a small information block in a premium catalogue rather than advertising copy.

Position it independently from the main headline, preferably toward the upper-right or another natural negative-space area.

DISTRIBUTED GRAPHIC SYSTEM

Do not place all typography at the top.

Use the entire 4:5 frame as a structured editorial grid.

Graphic elements may appear in:

upper-left
upper-center
upper-right
lower-left
lower-right

depending on available negative space.

Use only a few elements.

Possible elements include:

thin horizontal rules
very thin vertical rules
small corner brackets
tiny catalogue numbering
subtle registration-style marks
small Woolworths brand symbols

These elements should create visual tension and structure, not decoration.

BRAND SIGNATURE

Include a small, accurate Woolworths brand signature.

Prefer a natural position near the upper-left or upper-right portion of the composition.

The brand signature should be clearly identifiable but never dominate the headline or photograph.

Woolworths Green should remain the primary brand accent.

Do not introduce additional strong colours merely for graphic decoration.

GRAPHIC / PHOTOGRAPH RELATIONSHIP

Typography should interact with the architecture and negative space of the photograph.

Where possible:

align text with architectural lines
use shelf or ceiling geometry as compositional guides
allow graphic rules to echo perspective lines
place text in naturally quieter areas
never cover important facial features
never obscure the trolley
never obscure important products

The graphic system should feel as though the photographer and graphic designer designed the composition together before the photograph was made.

TYPOGRAPHIC HIERARCHY

The hierarchy should be:

1. Hero photograph

2. Trolley + human story

3. Primary headline

4. Woolworths brand

5. Catalogue identifier

6. Supporting/value information

7. Technical graphic details

The photograph must remain dominant.

The typography should make the photograph feel more like a campaign, not compete with it.

IMPORTANT — DESIGN DIRECTION

The result should feel closer to a real premium retail catalogue cover than to a conventional supermarket advertisement.

Avoid:

centered headline
thin luxury-magazine typography
large empty banner at the top
text confined entirely to the top edge
generic corporate presentation
excessive copy
poster-like advertising
fashion-magazine clichés

Instead use:

bold editorial typography + asymmetric composition + restrained information blocks + distributed graphic anchors.

FINAL DESIGN TEST

At thumbnail size, the image should still immediately read as:

a premium Woolworths campaign photograph.

At larger size, the viewer should discover:

the catalogue system → headline → brand → information details.

The graphic design must reward closer inspection without overwhelming the photograph.

ABSOLUTELY AVOID
Avoid generic stock-photo supermarket scenes, staged advertising poses, people looking at camera, exaggerated smiles, perfectly symmetrical aisles, unrealistic supermarket architecture, luxury-mall aesthetics, futuristic retail design, excessive green, excessive bokeh, fisheye distortion, extreme wide-angle distortion, artificial glow, excessive lens flare, fake HDR, oversaturation, plastic skin, CGI-looking materials, impossible trolley geometry, floating objects, duplicated products, duplicated people, distorted hands, malformed bodies, warped shelving, nonsensical packaging, fake or distorted branding, and visible generative artifacts.
Do not make the image look like an AI concept render.
Do not turn the supermarket into a futuristic or luxury environment.
Do not make the trolley physically impossible.

FINAL OUTPUT
4:5 vertical. Photorealistic premium commercial photography combined with a sophisticated asymmetric editorial catalogue system. One coherent photographic world, one hero story, one hero trolley, one unmistakably Woolworths identity, and a confident but restrained graphic language.
