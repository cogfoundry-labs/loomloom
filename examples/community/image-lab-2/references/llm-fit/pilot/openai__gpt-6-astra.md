## Direction cards

### 1 — Quiet introduction
**Idea:** A straightforward, softly lit studio portrait of the bottle, preserving your brief without adding a narrative.

- **Picture:** Photographic bottle on a seamless warm-white tabletop; a soft amber shadow anchors it. Complete bottle and readable label, with generous breathing room.
- **Composition and type:** Moderate product scale, restrained single-line headline, simple vertical hierarchy. Soft directional light; smooth matte finish.
- **Emotional intent:** Calm, candid, quietly premium.
- **Insight served — assumption:** A clear view of the actual package can feel more credible than elaborate beauty-advertising symbolism.
- **Difference:** The neutral baseline: ordinary perspective, minimal staging, no environmental story.
- **Keep out:** No scenery, decorative props, hard theatrical shadows or scientific imagery.
- **Copy:** Headline **SKIN, CLEARLY.**; label **HALDEN**.
- **Fixed:** Seamless studio setting, soft photographic treatment, graphite accent confined to the headline.
- **Varies:** Three studio compositions; warm-white versus soft-amber ground dominance; broad side light versus soft frontal light.

### 2 — At home in daylight
**Idea:** The serum belongs in a quiet, believable daily setting.

- **Picture:** Full-bleed interior photograph, with the bottle physically resting on a warm-white stone ledge beside a window. One muted-olive ceramic plane sits behind it, out of focus; no cutout.
- **Composition and type:** Environmental perspective and a smaller product within the scene, but a clearly readable label. Small headline in open wall space. Natural daylight and tactile surfaces.
- **Emotional intent:** Familiar, grounded, unforced.
- **Insight served — assumption:** Everyday context may reassure an audience that distrusts beauty-advertising spectacle.
- **Difference:** The only lived-in environmental image; depth, window architecture and natural material replace the seamless studio.
- **Keep out:** No bathroom clutter, mirrors, foliage, people, lifestyle claims or oversized headline.
- **Copy:** Headline **SKIN, CLEARLY.**; label **HALDEN**.
- **Fixed:** Full-bleed photographic scene, warm-white stone, one olive accent, small headline.
- **Varies:** Three scene-specific viewpoints; warm-white versus soft-amber ground dominance; diffuse daylight versus a broad sun patch.

### 3 — Plainly printed
**Idea:** A restrained editorial announcement built from paper, space and a sharply photographed product.

- **Picture:** A faithful photographic bottle integrated into a visibly flat, matte-paper composition. Warm white, soft amber and one slate-blue paper shape.
- **Composition and type:** Orthographic graphic structure rather than a room or tabletop horizon. Large but light-weight headline, never overlapping the bottle. Two broad paper fields at most.
- **Emotional intent:** Clear, deliberate, design-conscious without shouting.
- **Insight served — assumption:** Editorial order can communicate confidence without relying on clinical cues or benefit claims.
- **Difference:** Deliberately contrasts with the photographic baseline through flat construction, a larger headline and graphic geometry.
- **Keep out:** No drawn bottle, distressed label, diagrams, data marks, badges, extra copy or dense collage.
- **Copy:** Headline **SKIN, CLEARLY.**; label **HALDEN**.
- **Fixed:** Photographic product, flat paper construction, slate-blue accent, large light-weight headline.
- **Varies:** Three poster-specific arrangements; warm-white versus soft-amber ground dominance; smooth versus visibly fibrous paper finish.

### 4 — Light, held still
**Idea:** A single bottle becomes a quiet architectural still life.

- **Picture:** A sharply photographed bottle in a shallow warm-white plaster niche. Light creates a broad soft-amber shadow field; a muted-ochre recess edge is the sole accent.
- **Composition and type:** Bottle framed by architectural planes, with **at least one-third of the frame left empty**. Very small headline in that open area. Label receives enough neutral fill to remain white and readable.
- **Emotional intent:** Contemplative, intimate, slightly luxurious.
- **Insight served — assumption:** Space and carefully controlled light may suggest care without ostentation.
- **Difference:** Light and architectural emptiness carry the image; unlike Direction 1, this is a spatial, shadow-led composition rather than neutral presentation.
- **Keep out:** No floating bottle, dramatic black void, water effects, spotlight glare, unreadable label or elaborate set dressing.
- **Copy:** Headline **SKIN, CLEARLY.**; label **HALDEN**.
- **Fixed:** Plaster niche, ochre accent, broad amber shadow, very small headline, at least one-third negative space.
- **Varies:** Three architectural compositions; warm-white versus soft-amber ground dominance; diagonal versus vertical shadow structure.

**No wildcard is proposed. All four directions retain every hard constraint.**
