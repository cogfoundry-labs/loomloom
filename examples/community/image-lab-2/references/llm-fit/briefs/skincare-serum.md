Launch key visual for HALDEN, a small skincare brand, announcing its first product: a vitamin C and niacinamide face serum in a 30 ml amber glass bottle with a black dropper and a plain white label reading HALDEN in capital letters.

Audience: women and men aged 25 to 40 who distrust loud beauty advertising and want something calm and credible. Objective: the pre-launch image for Instagram and the top of the website, portrait 4:5.

Headline: SKIN, CLEARLY. That is the only text besides the label.

Mandatory: the bottle is always visible, in focus and unaltered, label readable. No faces (hands are fine). No before and after, no clinical or medical claims, no percentages, no white lab coats. The palette is built on warm white and soft amber with one accent color; never pink. Mood: quiet, honest, a little luxurious, never clinical.
