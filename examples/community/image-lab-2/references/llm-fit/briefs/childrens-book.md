Cover art for PIP AND THE PAPER BOATS, a picture book for children aged 3 to 6 from a small independent publisher. The hero is Pip, a small round hedgehog in a red knitted scarf, who sails folded paper boats down a rain-filled gutter on a grey day and discovers the boats carry small lights.

Audience: parents and grandparents choosing a gift, and the children themselves. Objective: the front cover and the key image for the publisher's catalogue, portrait 3:4.

Title: PIP AND THE PAPER BOATS. It is the only text on the cover.

Mandatory: Pip looks the same on every image (round body, red knitted scarf, small dark eyes, no clothes besides the scarf). No human faces, no violence, no scary shadows, no brands or logos. The palette is built on rain-washed blue-grey and butter yellow, with one accent color. Mood: gentle, curious, a little magical, never saccharine.
